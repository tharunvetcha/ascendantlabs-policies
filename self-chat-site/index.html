A few helpful answers

Before you begin.

A little clarity about what Self Chat is designed to be.

What is Self Chat?

Self Chat is a private space to message yourself — text, photos, voice notes, and documents, organized into as many threads as you want, styled like the messaging app you already use.

Is it a messaging app?

No. Self Chat is a personal space where you write messages to yourself. It is not a messaging service for chatting with other people — there's no one else on it.

Does it need an internet connection?

No. Self Chat never makes a network request. Every message, photo, and voice note you save lives only in a local database on your device.
