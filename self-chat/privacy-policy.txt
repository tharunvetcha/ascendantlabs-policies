Self Chat — Privacy Policy
Last updated: October 15, 2026

Short version: Self Chat does not collect, store, transmit, or share any of your data with anyone — including the developer. Everything you write stays on your device, in a local database, forever.

What Self Chat is

Self Chat is a personal journaling app styled like a messaging app, where every "chat" is a thread you write to yourself. It is designed to work 100% offline.

Data collection

Self Chat does not have a backend server, does not make network requests, does not use analytics or crash-reporting SDKs, and does not show ads. There is no account to create and no sign-in. Nothing you type, attach, or do inside the app is ever sent anywhere.

Where your data lives

All chats, messages, and attachments (photos, videos, voice notes, documents) are stored in a local SQLite database and local file storage on your device only. If you uninstall the app, back up your data first using the in-app "Backup & restore" feature (Settings) — the exported file is yours to store or share however you choose; the app itself never uploads it anywhere.

Permissions the app may request

• Notifications — to show reminders you set on your own messages.
• Camera — only when you choose to take a photo or video from inside the app.
• Photos / videos / files — only when you choose to attach existing media to a message.
• Microphone — when you choose to record a voice note, and whenever you open the in-app camera (it requests microphone access up front in case you decide to record a video).
• Alarms & reminders (Android 12+) — to schedule reminder notifications at the exact time you pick.

Each of these is used solely to provide the corresponding in-app feature and never leaves your device.

Third parties

Self Chat does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs, and does not share data with any third party, because it does not collect any data to share in the first place.

Children's privacy

Self Chat does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children, since it collects no information at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be published at this same URL with a new "Last updated" date.
